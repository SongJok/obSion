# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Obsion community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing
viewpoints, accountability, and a focus on what is best for the community. Unacceptable
behavior includes harassment, discrimination, sexualized attention, intimidation,
trolling, personal or political attacks, and publishing another person's private
information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communication that
do not align with this code. Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be
reported privately through the security contact described in `SECURITY.md`. All
reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with confidentiality appropriate to the
situation.
